# Bibliography and Further Reading

Sources are grouped by theme and, within themes, roughly by importance for a reader who wants to go deeper. This list emphasizes accessible authoritative sources over original papers where both exist. URLs are omitted for stability; all items are findable by title. Items marked ★ are the ones the author would recommend first.

## Continuously updated trackers (check these before trusting any number in this document)

- ★ **Epoch AI**, *Trends in AI*; *Data on AI Models*; *Machine Learning Hardware*; *Capabilities Index*; *FrontierMath*. The most careful public quantitative record of compute, cost, hardware, and capability.
- ★ **METR**, *Task-Completion Time Horizons of Frontier AI Models* (live dashboard); Kwa et al., "Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks" (2025); "Time Horizon 1.1" (January 2026).
