## D. How to update this document

This review will be wrong in specifics within months. To update it:

1. Check the trackers in Chapter 22's first section (Epoch, METR, AI Index, International AI Safety Report, Stanford indicators).
2. Compare the forecast register (Section A) against events; note which resolved and how.
3. Watch the signposts in Chapters 17 and 18.
4. Revise the scenario probabilities (Chapter 18) explicitly rather than implicitly.

The source is maintained at github.com/gorg667/ai-future. Corrections and updates are welcome.
