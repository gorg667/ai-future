    Bibliography and Further Reading¶

    Sources are grouped by theme and, within themes, roughly by importance for a reader who wants to go deeper. This list emphasizes accessible authoritative sources over original papers where both exist. URLs are omitted for stability; all items are findable by title. Items marked ★ are the ones the author would recommend first.
